How to play

  • W A S D — move
  • Space — jump
  • Mouse — look around (Esc to unlock)
  • 1–4 tools · 5 dirt · 6 cobble · 7 log · 8 plank · 9 table
  • Look at a block or mob — its name appears under the crosshair
  • Hold left click — hit the mob you look at; if none, mine the block
  • Gold word cubes — break them for coins, HP, and word progress
  • Word gates block the path — walk up and answer to open them
  • C — 随身 2×2 合成；对着合成台右键打开 3×3 格子。左边点配方，或把材料摆进格子再点产物
  • Right click a placed table to open the full craft grid; otherwise place a block
  • Q — homing magic bolt; with a crafted bow and arrows it fires an arrow instead
  • V — Voice Challenge (say the word on the plaque). The world does not pause
  • Look at a mob and hold V — saying the English word crits or shatters the boss shield
  • T — type the word if the mic cannot hear you. Clicking Chinese is a fallback after two misses
  • F — talk to Merchant Leo (商人雷奥); sell loot or buy Leather Cap / Cloth Robe / Iron Blade / HP Potion
  • Gold word cubes and word gates still use a teaching card. Combat never pauses.
  • Wither takes damage when you hit it; blue shield only reduces damage
  • Say or type the word to break the shield; clicking Chinese only chips it
