# 2D 植物与僵尸透明爆炸图提示词

```text
Create an original 2D cartoon asset explosion sheet for a children's horizontal lawn tower-defense learning game. Use a consistent hand-painted 2D illustration language: thick nearly-black ink outlines, expressive faces, clean cel-like shading, warm highlights, slightly imperfect brush edges, and readable silhouettes. This must NOT be pixel art, voxel art, 3D render, photorealism, clay, or a copy of any existing game character.

Arrange 8 isolated complete assets in a clean 4 by 2 grid with generous spacing and no overlap:
1. a cheerful sunflower-like resource plant with a round golden face and two leaves;
2. a pea-shooter-like green plant with a large round muzzle;
3. a friendly wall-nut-like defensive plant with a tan shell and wide eyes;
4. a small blue ice plant with a snowflake accent;
5. an original basic zombie in a torn brown jacket and red tie, full body, walking pose;
6. an original conehead zombie with an orange traffic cone, full body;
7. an original buckethead zombie with a metal bucket, full body;
8. a round glowing sun token and three pea projectiles grouped as a small reward prop.

Background: perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key only. No shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation on the background. Do not use #00ff00 inside the assets. Keep every asset fully inside its cell, separated by at least 48px of green space. No text, no letters, no numbers, no Chinese characters, no pinyin, no fake writing, no logos, no watermark.
```
